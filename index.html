This 2018 corner-unit home in gated Sunset Heights offers 2,819 sf of upgrades: white oak hardwood floors, three levels of outdoor living, and a community pool. Four true ensuite bedrooms flex for guests, in-laws, home offices, or roommates; the first-floor suite is no-stairs accessible and an elevator shaft is already framed in.

Tech includes EV charger, hardwired ethernet, zoned AC per floor, smart thermostats, Ring entry, and tankless hot water. Inside: 12-ft ceilings, Bosch appliances, quartz counters, gas fireplace, and a primary suite with marble shower, soaking tub, and walk-in closet.

The fourth-floor game room opens to an oversized rooftop terrace; the second-floor balcony has a gas grill connection. Gated but facing streetside on Harvard, allowing for easy navigation. Walk to Heights retail and dining; zoned to A-rated Helms Elementary, Hamilton Middle School, and Heights High School. Newer than nearly every comparable. The Heights

Sunset Heights sits at the heart of Houston's most walkable urban neighborhood. Step outside the gate and you're a short stroll from 19th and 20th Street's independent restaurants, boutiques, and coffee shops — plus the Heights Farmers Market on Saturdays.

The M-K-T trail system connects through White Oak Bayou greenspace directly from the neighborhood, and Heights Boulevard's landmark tree-lined esplanade is minutes away on foot. Yale Street's Whole Foods, Target, and restaurants handle everyday errands without a highway.

Zoned to A-rated Helms Elementary, Hamilton Middle School (walkable), and Heights High School. Quick access to I-10 and Loop 610 puts Downtown, the Galleria, and both airports within easy reach.
